## What the inspected opening pages establish

The abstract says the authors analyzed 27 isothermal experimental PXY datasets, comprising 284 points, at pressures up to 2 MPa. The paper investigates temperature dependence of Wilson-equation coefficients and compares calculated results against several categories of published VLE data.

The introduction distinguishes datasets that report pressure, temperature, and both liquid- and vapor-phase compositions from data with less complete composition information. It also identifies earlier studies that contribute isothermal datasets. Thus, the paper is a synthesis/model-analysis source; the origin of each underlying measurement must be tracked to its cited primary study.

The inspected text describes an objective function based on excess Gibbs free energy and equations for Wilson activity coefficients. The complete method, parameter tables, data-selection details, and applicability limits have not yet been reviewed.

## Relevance and limits for this project

This paper may help understand low-to-moderate-pressure aqueous-ammonia VLE data and the treatment of non-ideal liquid-phase behavior. The abstract states an estimation procedure for conditions with pressure below 2 MPa and vapor ammonia mole fraction below 0.9. Applicability to the project's high-side conditions must therefore be assessed rather than assumed.

The project must not use this source as a substitute for inspecting the original experimental dataset when validating the existing teqp comparison. The paper's compiled data, fitted Wilson parameters, and the original measurements are distinct evidence types.
