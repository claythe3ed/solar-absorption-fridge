===============================================================================================
  INTERPRETATION
===============================================================================================

  - Ziegler-Trepp and teqp should agree within ~1 C for the mixture
    if both models are physically correct.
  - y_NH3 (vapor composition) tells us how selective the boiling is.
    Higher y = more ammonia enriched in vapor.
  - Liquid/vapor density ratio should be ~100-300x for a typical
    absorption cycle operating point.
  - Any disagreement > 2 C indicates either:
    * A real physical difference between models
    * A convergence issue in either solver
    * A unit conversion error
