## What the paper actually demonstrates

The study reports experimental operation of a solar thermal NH3/H2O absorption refrigeration system. The chiller was designed at ITW and a demonstration system was built/tested at KFUPM under Saudi Arabian climatic conditions.

The reported system includes:

- generator
- dephlegmator / rectification function
- condenser
- evaporator
- absorber
- internal and external heat exchangers
- refrigerant expansion valves
- solution expansion valves
- mechanically driven solution pump

The generator assembly contains heating coils, heat-recovery coils, and a rectification zone. The rectification zone uses stainless-steel packing to promote heat and mass transfer between hot vapor and cooler strong solution.

3. Continuous operation with a solution pump.
4. Experimentally demonstrated.
5. Tested with high heat-rejection temperature.
6. Demonstrated at sub-zero evaporator temperature.
