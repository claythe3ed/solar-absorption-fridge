## Technical information from the inspected pages

The guideline says the formulation represents thermodynamic properties of fluid phases in ammonia–water mixtures and refers to the paper by Tillner-Roth and Friend, “A Helmholtz Free Energy Formulation of the Thermodynamic Properties of the Mixture {Water + Ammonia}.”

The nomenclature identifies the ammonia mole fraction, molar Helmholtz free energy, pressure, temperature, density, enthalpy, entropy, fugacity coefficients, and bubble/dew quantities. The guideline also states that the formulation was constrained to match specified pure-water and pure-ammonia property surfaces in the pure-fluid limits, subject to small differences described in the document.

This is only an identification-level summary. The formulation equations, coefficients, validity range, and implementation details have not yet been checked against the full guideline.
