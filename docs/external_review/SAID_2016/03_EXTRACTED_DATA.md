## Architecture evidence

The experimental chiller uses a mechanically circulated solution loop. The paper explicitly identifies a diaphragm solution pump.

The generator assembly includes:

1. heating coils,
2. heat-recovery coils,
3. rectification zone.

The rectification zone contains stainless-steel packing.

The vapor path includes dephlegmation/rectification before condensation. The paper's description says undesirable water and a small amount of ammonia condense and return, while nearly pure ammonia vapor leaves the dephlegmator.

## What is not extracted/approved from this paper

Do not infer the following without inspecting the paper's equations/instrumentation in detail:

- exact COP numerator/denominator equation;
- exact generator heat-metering boundary;
- exact uncertainty budget for COP;
- exact NH3/H2O concentrations at each measured point;
- exact refrigerant mass-flow rate;
- exact pump electrical consumption;
- exact pressure data;
- exact pressure-vessel design basis;
- exact material specifications for every wetted part;
- exact solar collector optical model;
- exact heat-transfer coefficients.

These are separate audit targets.
